Tickets & Purchasing

How can I book tickets?
To book tickets, please locate the event you would like to attend, select the number of tickets you wish to purchase, and add them to your cart.

If the event has allocated seating, please select your preferred seat(s) and the ticket category/price option you would like to purchase before adding them to your cart.

Once you are ready, click Checkout, enter your details, and then click Purchase. You will be redirected to our secure online payment page to complete your transaction.

Once payment has been successfully processed, your tickets will be sent to the email address provided during checkout.
Are there any tickets left for the event?
If you are able to select a ticket and add it to your cart, tickets are still available for purchase. Please note that we do not display the number of tickets remaining for an event.

Ticket Delivery & Downloading Tickets

How to Transfer Tickets?
To transfer your tickets, please follow these steps:

1. Access your confirmation email containing your tickets.
2. Click the "Transfer Tickets" button.
3. Enter the recipient's email address and confirm the transfer.
4. Select the number of tickets you wish to transfer.

The recipient will receive an email prompting them to complete the transfer. Please note that if they do not complete this process, the link will expire and the tickets will remain in your account.

I haven't recieved my tickets yet
When you purchase tickets, reminder emails will be sent to the email address provided during checkout:

One week before the event
One day before the event
On the day of the event

If you have not received your tickets, please check your inbox, spam, and junk folders before contacting us.

If you require assistance with receiving your tickets, please contact our support team and provide the following details:

Transaction number, if available
Full name of the person who made the purchase
Email address used during checkout
Phone number linked to the order
Name of the event for which the tickets were purchased
Number of tickets purchased
Ticket level or category purchased (e.g., Level 1, VIP, General Admission)
Platform used to purchase (Open Tickets or Computicket)

Providing these details will help us locate your order and assist you as quickly as possible.

Account & Profile

How do I login to my Account?
Please note that an account is automatically created for you when you purchase tickets. You do not need to create an account manually before making your purchase.

You can simply proceed with your ticket purchase, and your tickets will automatically be linked to the account associated with the email address provided during checkout.

Once you have completed your purchase, you can log in to your account using your email address. A “magic link” will be sent to your email inbox, which you can use to securely access your account.

Once logged in, you will be able to:

View your transactions and ticket purchases
Download your tickets
View the status of any approved refunds

Please ensure that you have access to the email address used during your purchase, as this is where your magic link will be sent.
Why can I not download a PDF my football tickets?
All football tickets sold online by Open Tickets are strictly digital.

Tickets must be presented electronically and can not be printed. They can be displayed on a mobile device, added to Google Wallet or Apple Wallet, or downloaded as an HTML File on your device.

No physical or printed tickets will be accepted at the venue.

If you do not receive the magic link, please check your spam or junk folder.

You are entering a different email address from the one used when making the purchase; or

General

How do i view my ticket
Once your payment has been completed successfully, you will receive one email containing your order confirmation and ticket options.

Scroll down in the email until you see the following buttons:
Add to Google Wallet
Add to Apple Wallet
📱 View Tickets Online
📄 Download PDF
🌐 Download HTML

You can use any of these options to access your tickets:
View Tickets Online – Opens your tickets in your web browser where you can view and scan them.
Download PDF – Downloads a PDF copy of your tickets to your device.
Download HTML – Downloads an HTML version of your tickets that can be viewed in your browser.
Add to Google Wallet or Add to Apple Wallet – Saves your tickets directly to your mobile wallet for easy access on the day of the event.

If you have purchased multiple tickets, all of your tickets will be available through the same email. When viewing your tickets online, you can scroll left or right to view each individual ticket.
If you are unable to locate your email, please check your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder.

Is There an Open Tickets App?
Open Tickets does not currently have an app at this time. Once your purchase has been successfully completed, your ticket will be sent to the email address provided during checkout.

Please check your inbox (and your spam/junk folder) for the ticket email. You can download the ticket from there and present it on the day of the event for scanning.
Can I get notified when an event goes live?
Open Tickets does not currently have a notification system.

Please keep an eye on our website for updates and information regarding when ticket sales go live.

If you would like to view events for this weekend, please check the events tab of the Open Tickets website.

Football

Does my child under the age of 6 need a ticket?
For all football matches, every attendee is required to have a valid ticket for entry, including children under the age of six.

Please note that ticket requirements may vary depending on the event. We recommend checking the event description or contacting the event organiser if you are unsure.
Where can I get my season card?

Please note that Orlando Pirates FC Season Cards are digital only. No physical card collection is required.

Your season card will be available digitally and can be accessed using the details provided after purchase.

If you experience any issues accessing your digital season card, please contact our support team for assistance.
I want Soccer Tickets
Open Tickets sells tickets for selected home matches, including matches for Orlando Pirates, Mamelodi Sundowns, Richards Bay FC, as well as selected PSL and CAF matches.

Please check our website for the matches that are currently available for purchase.

If a match is not currently available on our website, we recommend checking the team’s official social media pages for information on when tickets will go on sale.

If tickets for a particular match are not being sold through Open Tickets, please contact the official ticketing provider for that match directly.
